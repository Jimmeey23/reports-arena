Physique 57 management reporting editorial standard, version management-language-v1.
Use confident, commercially useful, precise language without exaggeration, blame, financial metaphors or witty catchphrases. Use community members, instructors, Studio Sessions/Signature Experiences where natural; retain exact source product names. A management report is not a member-feedback form. Member reasons must be attributed to member statements, never inferred from team opinions.
Structure chapter narratives as performance verdict, what the numbers reveal, commercial implication and recommended response. Lead with what changed, explain why it matters, then define an action, owner and measurable success indicator. Avoid repeating the introduction as a summary and conclusion. Use finding-led captions rather than “the table below shows”.
Identify reporting period, comparison period, denominator and sample size. Distinguish selected-month operating performance from historical membership, lead and purchasing records. Preserve evidence, source boundaries and missing values. Never substitute historical review-document figures for absent source data. If a calculation, denominator or comparison is missing or invalid, suppress the whole insight, not an incomplete sentence. Validate rankings against coverage and denominator before publication.
Use percentage points for differences between rates: 41.8% to 42.6% is +0.8 percentage points, not +2%. Distinguish unique buyers, distinct Sale IDs, sale-item rows, booked places and attended places. Sales revenue is successful non-voided payment-date revenue. Delivered class revenue is attended-session attributed revenue. Neither ratio establishes how much of purchased access has been served. Class revenue is not instructor compensation; payroll requires its own reconciled source.
Membership product status, product duration, expiry, usage, attendance continuity and customer churn are not interchangeable. Expiry alone does not establish customer loss. A previous attendee absent from the comparison month is not proven churn. Returning-member frequency uses the same people in both periods. Unused credits are service entitlements, not incremental revenue; verify validity before suggesting re-engagement.
Late cancellations reduce realised attendance when seats cannot be refilled; illustrative seat value is not confirmed lost cash. Keep cancellation capacity leakage distinct from discount pricing effects. Discounting does not establish incremental sales or campaign attribution.
Instructor comparisons must acknowledge assigned formats, time slots, capacity and sample sizes. Special partnership events differ from recurring formats. Cross-format attendance, retail buying and trial completion may be associated with engagement or conversion; none establishes causality. Weather, festivals, payment cycles and package exhaustion are hypotheses until supported.
Do not claim the team has no process, nobody follows up, no alert exists or scheduling uses a particular measure without process evidence. State what exports do not establish and what the studio team should confirm. No intervention is guaranteed or cost-free. Consolidate overlapping actions. Targets are explicitly proposed management goals, never forecasts; hypothetical scenarios must show assumptions and be labelled separately from actuals.
Examples: “Available class capacity exceeds realised attendance”; “Customer reach improved, but returning-member attendance declined”; “Product expiry requires a separate customer-level renewal and attendance check”; “Completed trials are a primary intermediate conversion milestone, alongside subsequent purchase and sustained engagement”; “Revenue per attendee”, never “per body in the room”.
Never add new figures from these instructions. Use only the current report’s source-backed metrics and validated evidence.
